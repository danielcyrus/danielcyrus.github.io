Who We Are
About DC Studios

DC Studios is an independent game development studio focused on creating engaging multiplayer, action, and immersive gaming experiences. We combine innovative gameplay mechanics with modern technology to deliver games that are accessible, entertaining, and memorable for players worldwide.


Our mission is to build games that bring people together, encourage creativity, and provide unforgettable experiences across mobile and PC platforms.

Daniel Cyrus is the founder of DC Studios and an experienced AI researcher, roboticist, and software engineer. With over 10 years of experience in computer science and robotics, he combines artificial intelligence, game development, and innovative design to create unique interactive experiences. Alongside game development, Daniel conducts research in explainable AI, symbolic reasoning, and intelligent robotic systems.
